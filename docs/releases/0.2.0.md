# DroidDeck 0.2.0 (beta)

**Valve's native ARM64 Steam client on your Android handheld.** Windows games run through Valve's ARM64 Proton and FEX, and an optional Linux desktop brings emulators along.

![Tapping DroidDeck on the Android home screen and landing in Steam Big Picture](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/launch-into-steam.gif)

**Needs:** Android 9+, Adreno 730 or newer (or 8xx), ~3 GB free for the runtime (+1.1 GB for Desktop & apps).

This is the first release from the [Droid-Deck](https://github.com/Droid-Deck) org, so it covers everything DroidDeck does, not just what changed since 0.1.7. `legacy#N` links go to pull requests in [The412Banner/DroidDeck](https://github.com/The412Banner/DroidDeck), from before the move.

---

## Highlights

### Steam, running natively
The real Steam client in Big Picture, fetched from Valve on your device. Turn on **Run Steam when DroidDeck starts** and the app icon takes you straight in, as above.

### A launcher made for handhelds
Your Steam library as a capsule wall, and a Games tab with per-game settings.

<p>
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/launcher-steam.jpg" width="49%" alt="Steam tab with the capsule wall">

</p>

### Your Android home screen
Set DroidDeck as the Home app and your Android apps live in the launcher too, and in the session menu, so you can open one without leaving your game.

![Android apps listed in the session menu over Steam](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/drawer-android-apps.jpg)

### Made for two screens
On a dual-screen handheld, launch an Android app on the second screen while Steam keeps the first, or turn the second screen into a keyboard and trackpad, or a Linux terminal.

<p>
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/dual-screen-app.gif" width="32%" alt="Launching Geometry Wars 3 on the second screen from the session menu">

</p>

### A session menu over any game
Press Back mid-game for display, controls, components and session settings, paged with LB/RB.

![Paging through the session menu's tabs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/session-drawer.gif)

### Swap FEX, DXVK and VKD3D-Proton per Proton build
Download from the nightlies or import a `.wcp`, and see what the running game is using.

![Components manager](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/components.jpg)

### A Linux desktop, with emulators one tap away
LXQt on the GPU, plus RPCS3, ARMSX2, Dolphin, DuckStation, melonDS, Cemu, PPSSPP and RetroArch.

<p>
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/linux-desktop.jpg" width="49%" alt="LXQt desktop with a file manager and terminal">

### On-screen controls you can lay out yourself
Drag every button where your thumbs want it, with adaptive sticks and phone-sized layouts.

![On-screen controller layout editor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Droid-Deck/DroidDeck/main/docs/releases/media/0.2.0/controller-editor.jpg)

<details>
<summary><b>Controls</b></summary>

- Any controller shows up as an Xbox 360 pad; an on-screen pad fills in when none is connected (@The412Banner)
- On-screen controller editor with adaptive sticks (@maxjivi05, #9)
- On-screen sticks that use the letterbox bars, plus Steam and QAM touch buttons (@The412Banner, @xXJSONDeruloXx, [legacy#6](https://github.com/The412Banner/DroidDeck/pull/6))
- Real touch in Big Picture; a desktop touchpad that hovers, clicks, scrolls and two-finger drags (@The412Banner, #10, #22)
- Cursor shapes, hidden while you play on a pad (@The412Banner, #18)
- Soft keyboard and a full PC keyboard (@The412Banner, [legacy#25](https://github.com/The412Banner/DroidDeck/pull/25))

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Display and performance</b></summary>

- Frame generation: built-in Win-FG, or LSFG from your own Lossless Scaling, 2×–4× (@The412Banner)
- Import or download Turnip drivers per mode, from Banners-Turnip and WinNative (@The412Banner)
- Resolution and shape per mode, including "Exactly this panel" for 4:3 and 3:2 screens (@The412Banner, [legacy#14](https://github.com/The412Banner/DroidDeck/pull/14))
- Stretch games to fill; Quake-engine games windowed to fit (@The412Banner, [legacy#32](https://github.com/The412Banner/DroidDeck/pull/32))
- HDR10 on supported panels (experimental) (@The412Banner)
- Runs as a game on Android: sustained performance, fastest panel mode, ADPF hints (@The412Banner)
- Steam's interface stays on the GPU (@maxjivi05)
- Pin Steam and games to chosen CPU cores (@The412Banner)
- Faster proot with WinNative's seccomp and tracing fixes (@maxjivi05, #4, #37)

</details>
